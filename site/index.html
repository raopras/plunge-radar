How Agile Octopus pricing works

Agile Octopus charges a different price for every half-hour, linked to the wholesale market. Octopus Energy publishes tomorrow's prices between 4pm and 8pm, usually nearer 4pm.

Prices are often cheaper when the grid is greener, and usually most expensive from 4pm to 7pm. When more electricity is generated than used, prices can drop below zero and customers are paid for the electricity they use. At peak times Agile can cost more than the price cap rate, so it suits homes that can move their usage.
